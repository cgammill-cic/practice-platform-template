Four ways to charge

Each shows what you earn per hour of your time and what the client saves. The strongest option for these numbers is outlined.

How the numbers are built. Floor = your hours × your rate × the risk multiplier, plus tools. The fair range starts at the floor and tops out at the lower of 40% of the traditional build or half of the value over the years it lasts. Effective rate = what you're paid ÷ the hours it costs you.

The rates, CRM costs and hour estimates are rules of thumb, not quotes. Check them against a couple of local bids before you put a number in a proposal. Your entries are saved in this browser only.
